Our Story

Two people, one flat-top, a lot of shouting

We started in 2019 with a second-hand grill in a Fitzroy car park and a rule we still keep: if it doesn't have a lacy, crispy edge, it doesn't leave the pass.

Beef is dry-aged 28 days and ground twice a day. Buns come out of our own oven at 5am. The pickles ferment for three weeks, which is roughly how long it took to get the recipe right.

No table service, no reservations, no truffle oil. Just a good burger, done properly, at a price that doesn't need a special occasion.
